Alex lived at the edge of a big forest. He had a house, food, and a phone, but he had no friends. Every day, he walked alone, and every day, the forest felt a little quieter.

One day, Alex tripped and fell. His apples rolled everywhere. "Oh no... now I have to do everything alone again," he said.

Then, a small hand picked up an apple. "You dropped this! Are you okay?" asked Milo the Monkey. Alex had never talked to an animal before. But Milo didn't care. He just helped. And for the first time in a long time, Alex laughed.

"Thank you! I thought I had to do everything alone."
"In the forest, no one does anything alone!" said Milo.

On the way home, they found Tia the Turtle, stuck on her back. Milo wanted to run fast. Alex wanted to leave her. But Milo said, "Wait. Friends don't leave friends behind."

So they pushed together. Slowly. Very slowly. Until Tia was free.
"Thank you... You are... very... kind..." said Tia.

Then the storm came. A big tree fell and blocked the road home. Alex was scared. Milo was too small. Tia was too slow. They needed help.

"Need a hand? Or... a paw?" said Bruno the Bear.

Bruno was strong. Milo was fast. Tia was patient. And Alex had an idea. Together, they pushed. Together, they moved the tree. Together, they were stronger than any storm.

That evening, Alex didn't walk home alone. He walked home with three friends.
"I came to the forest looking for apples. I found something better," said Alex.
"Friends!" they all shouted.

A true friend is not the one who is fastest or strongest. A true friend is the one who stays when the storm comes.
