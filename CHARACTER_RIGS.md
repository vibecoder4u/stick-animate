# Species-specific character rigs

The renderer now has separate procedural body plans for:
- Human (existing articulated upright skeleton)
- Dog and cat (quadruped spine, four legs, muzzle/ears, tail)
- Bird (feathered torso, beak, wings, legs)
- Fish (horizontal body, tail fin, pectoral/dorsal fins, gill marks)
- Dinosaur / T-rex (bipedal hind limbs, balancing tail, skull, forearms, dorsal ridge)

Each non-human rig has its own proportions and animation parameters. These are
procedural starter rigs, not finished studio-quality character art or a general
skeletal mesh/IK system. New species can be added as independent rig modules.
